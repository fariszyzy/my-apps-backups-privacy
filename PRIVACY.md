# Privacy Policy — My Apps Backups

_Last updated: 2026-11-14_

## Overview

**My Apps Backups** is a personal backup application used to store and synchronize application backups in your own Google Drive account.

## Data Accessed

When you authorize the application with Google OAuth, it requests only the non-sensitive `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` scope:

- Access is limited strictly to the specific Google Drive files and folders created or opened by this application.
- The application does not access, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

## How Data Is Used

- Accessed data is used solely to upload, download, and synchronize your backups between your own devices/applications and your Google Drive.
- No personal data, analytics, telemetry, or backup contents are collected by, transmitted to, or stored on external developer servers.
- No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Your backup files remain solely in your Google Drive account under your control.
- You can delete your backup files directly from Google Drive at any time.
- You can revoke the application's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page: <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen or through <https://github.com/fariszyzy>.
